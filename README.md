# track-your-experience
Tell about your day, what you did, what contribution you made to the business, what your decision was, and something like that. 

We developers and engineers are very talented and also very busy tracking our awesome, excellent contribution to business. I am planning to make a thing. It could be software, a chatting bot, or anything else that will track your everyday activity and find out what is available. It will save it in the database.
Whenever you need it, for example:
- you want to rebuild your resume
- you want to apply for a job and prepare for an interview
- you want to hunt down your future contribution partner
you will ask this application. It will show and provide you with the available experience you have done before related to that man, that person, or that company.
